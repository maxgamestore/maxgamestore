🏢 About MaxGameStore

MaxGameStore Corporation is an indie game studio founded in 2025 with a mission to create games that connect players worldwide.

Our flagship title, World Of Fights, is a 2D multiplayer RPG where players fight, explore, level up, and build their legacy. We're committed to delivering a completely free experience with no pay-to-win mechanics.

We believe in transparency, community-driven development, and games that stand the test of time. Join us on this journey!
